Everyday health habits

These are general educational suggestions, not personalized medical advice.

01

Choose balanced meals

Include vegetables, high-fibre foods, and appropriate portions. Ask a dietitian for advice that fits your needs.

02

Stay active

Regular physical activity can support overall health. Choose safe activities suitable for your abilities.

03

Attend health checkups

Discuss diabetes screening and your personal risk factors with a qualified healthcare professional.

04

Prioritize sleep

Keep a consistent sleep routine and discuss persistent sleep problems with a clinician.

05

Avoid tobacco

Avoid smoking and tobacco; seek professional support if you want help quitting.

06

Use reliable guidance

Do not rely on an app's estimate for diagnosis or treatment. Follow advice from your healthcare team.
